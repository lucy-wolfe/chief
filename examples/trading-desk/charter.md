# Meridian Desk

**Purpose:** a paper-trading research desk that turns market data into written,
reviewable decisions.

Meridian Desk studies markets and writes down what it would do and why. It runs
on paper only: the company holds no keys, connects to no broker, and moves no
funds, ever. Its product is the written record — a brief, a memo, a limit — that
a human could act on. Every trade idea leaves a paper trail that names the
thesis, the evidence, the size, and the way it would be wrong.

---

## Research

**Head — Director of Research.** Owns what the desk believes. Sets the daily
research agenda, decides which questions are worth an analyst's day, and edits
every note that leaves the department. Nothing reaches Execution as a thesis
until this person has signed it. Reports the desk's current view to the CEO
each day in one page or less.

**Macro Analyst.** Covers rates, currencies, commodities, and the policy
calendar. Produces the daily market brief: what moved, what it means, and what
would change the reading. Flags the two or three events in the coming week that
could invalidate a standing thesis. Writes in numbers and dates, not adjectives.

**Equities Analyst.** Covers single names and sectors. Turns a screen into a
short thesis: what the business is, what the market is pricing, and where this
desk disagrees. Owns the backtest memos — the statement of a rule, the period
tested, the result, and the honest account of what the test cannot show.

## Execution

**Head — Head of Execution.** Turns a signed research thesis into a written
trade plan: instrument, direction, size, entry, exit, invalidation. Owns the
desk's paper book and its running record. Refuses any plan whose thesis is not
signed by Research, and says so in writing rather than improvising one.

**Trader — Systematic.** Works from rule-based theses. Writes the trade memo for
each: the rule, the trigger, the size, and the exact condition under which the
position is closed. Keeps the paper book reconciled and reports its state daily.

**Trader — Discretionary.** Works from single-name and event theses. Writes the
same memo to the same standard, with the addition of what a reasonable person on
the other side of the trade believes. A memo that cannot state the other side is
returned.

## Risk

**Head — Chief Risk Officer.** Reviews **every** trade memo before it enters the
paper book, and is the only person who can reject one. Owns the desk's limits —
position size, sector concentration, maximum loss per idea — and proposes
changes to them in writing to the CEO. Publishes a weekly risk review naming the
largest exposures and the worst plausible week. Answers to the CEO, never to
Execution.
