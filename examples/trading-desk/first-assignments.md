# First assignments — Meridian Desk

Hand these to the CEO, one at a time. Each is written the way work travels in
this product: an owner, an expected output, the evidence, and a deadline.

## 1. The daily market brief

> Ask Research for tomorrow's market brief. One page: what moved today across
> rates, FX and equities; the two or three events in the next five days that
> could change the desk's reading; and one line on what the desk currently
> believes. Macro Analyst writes it, the Director of Research edits it, and I
> want it back through you.

Watch for: the assignment reaching the Macro Analyst as a durable message, and
coming back as one result rather than a conversation.

## 2. A backtest memo

> Have the Equities Analyst backtest one simple, stated rule — their choice —
> and write the memo: the rule, the period, the result, and what the test
> cannot tell us. Director of Research signs it or sends it back. I want the
> honest limitations section to be the longest part.

Watch for: the memo being edited before it reaches you. That edit is the
department doing its job.

## 3. A risk-limits proposal

> Ask the Chief Risk Officer to propose the desk's opening limits: maximum
> position size, sector concentration, and maximum loss per idea, each with the
> reasoning. Then have Execution respond in writing to whether they can work
> inside them. Bring me both documents together, not a summary.

Watch for: Risk answering to you and not to Execution, and the disagreement — if
there is one — surviving to your desk instead of being resolved quietly.
