# First assignments — Signal & Co.

Hand these to the CEO, one at a time.

## 1. A 30-day content calendar

> Ask Content for a 30-day calendar for the launch: one row per day with the
> channel, the format, the working title, and the owner. Content Director sets
> the shape and edits it; the Copywriter fills it. Distribution reviews it for
> whether it can actually be shipped on that schedule and says so in writing.

Watch for: the calendar crossing a department boundary and coming back changed.
Distribution pushing back on Content is the company working.

## 2. A launch thread and post set

> Have the Copywriter write the launch announcement thread plus three
> supporting posts for a fictional product of the CEO's invention. Two options
> for the opening line. Designer specifies the visual for each. Content
> Director edits before any of it reaches me, and Distribution schedules it.

Watch for: the Content Director rejecting a claim the product cannot support.
That is the standing rule from the charter being enforced by a person.

## 3. The weekly metrics review format

> Ask Analytics to define the weekly review **before** anything ships: which
> numbers, from where, compared to what, and what would count as a
> disappointing week. I want the definition of failure written down while
> nobody knows the result yet.

Watch for: Analytics answering "we cannot tell from this" somewhere. A metrics
department that is never uncertain is not measuring anything.
